About Us

Saint Laurent Gaseke TVET School is a government-aided TVET institution based in Gicumbi District, Northern Province, Rwanda. Our mission is to provide high-quality technical and vocational training that equips students with the skills they need for employment, self-employment, and community development.

Our Mission

We strive to create a supportive and effective learning environment that meets student expectations and industry standards. Our competency-based approach gives learners practical skills that employers value.

Our Vision

We envision a future where every graduate from Saint Laurent Gaseke TVET School is a skilled, confident, and innovative professional contributing to national and regional development.

What We Offer

Our programs include:

  • Domestic Electricity – TVET Certificate Level 3
  • Industrial Electricity – TVET Certificates Levels 4 & 5
  • Electronic Services – TVET Certificates Levels 3 & 4
  • Computer Application – TVET Certificate Level 3
  • Software Development – TVET Certificate Level 4

Practical Training Approach

We emphasize hands-on learning that bridges classroom instruction and real industry tasks, ensuring students leave ready for technical careers or entrepreneurial paths.
